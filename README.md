# Guide to the controls:
Use the right / left arrows to move around,
the down arrow to crouch, (crouching reduces 7/8 of damage taken)
and the up arrow to jump.
Also, if you crouch and jump right after that you will do the crouch jump!
The crouch jump is higher than the normal jump.



# Guide to the two boss battles:
1st boss, Boom-boom:
   Okay, so yeah... basically all you have to do is stomp on the boss' head, right? WRONG! Also, after you hit Boom-boom, he will hide in
his SPIKED shell. So don't stomp on him then.

2nd boss, Bouldergeist:
   Just use your head in this one... By hitting the non-craked rocks with it!
Also, DO NOT stomp on him!!!
